## Design Context

### Users
Toss miniapp users reading saved Korean advanced-tarot consultations on phones. They need to revisit their question, cards, interpretation and clarifier comfortably.

### Brand Personality
Preserve the existing calm, friendly Boknyang cat identity shown in the user-provided screenshots. This task changes reading layout, not branding.

### Aesthetic Direction
Keep the existing light lavender surroundings, white reading surface, purple controls and illustrated cards. The user explicitly requests breathing room between titles, back buttons, paragraphs and images.

### Design Principles
- Separate sections clearly while grouping a card with its interpretation.
- Use short question headings; present long summaries as body text.
- Preserve every saved word, card and answer; improve presentation only.
- Keep controls readable and reachable on small mobile screens.
